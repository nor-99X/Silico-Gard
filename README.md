## 📌 Project Overview

Silico Guard is a fire safety innovation project that introduces two complementary silicone-based products:

1. **A silicone fire-extinguishing blanket** — designed to smother and extinguish fires, including fires that are difficult or unsafe to fight with conventional fiberglass blankets or water (e.g., oil and electrical fires).
2. **A self-cooling silicone blanket** — designed to provide immediate cooling and thermal protection for individuals exposed to high heat, such as during a fire or in harsh hot environments.

The project name, *SilcoGuard*, combines "**Silicone**" and "**Guard**," reflecting the material basis and protective purpose of the product.

Both concepts were carried from design through to physical prototypes, tested in practical (non-laboratory-certified) demonstrations described below.

---

## ⚠️ Problem Statement

Fires are among the greatest risks to the safety of people and property in workplaces, factories, homes, and vehicles, and can cause serious human and material losses. Conventional fire blankets present several limitations:

- They are not practical against all fire types (e.g., oil, electrical, and chemical fires) and can be costly due to rapid wear and damage.
- There is no single, reliable, and safe fire-response tool that reliably handles oil, electrical, and chemical fires.
- There is also a need for a way to protect individuals from high heat exposure during fires or in hot working environments — a need not addressed by traditional blankets, which are for fire suppression only.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Develop a new fire-extinguishing blanket made of heat-resistant silicone, as an alternative to traditional fiberglass blankets.
- Improve occupational safety standards in industrial and residential environments by providing a more effective fire-extinguishing tool.
- Reduce the risk of burns and injuries through a flexible design that is safe for direct human use.
- Extend the product's usable lifespan and reusability, making it more sustainable and cost-effective over time.
- Conduct practical tests comparing the silicone blanket's performance against traditional blankets.
- Develop a self-cooling blanket to protect individuals from high temperatures and provide comfort in harsh environments.

## 💡 Inspiration

The project idea originated while researching the uses of silicone across different industries, where its high heat resistance stood out. This led to the idea of using silicone to develop a fire-extinguishing blanket. Alongside this, the team identified a need for portable, easy-to-use cooling tools to protect individuals in harsh conditions — which led to the development of the self-cooling blanket.

## 🛡️ Safety Concept & Mechanism

### Silicone Fire-Extinguishing Blanket
- **Weighted edges:** Added weight along the edges increases the likelihood of a full seal, improving smothering effectiveness.
- **Oxygen isolation:** The blanket is placed over the fire source to cut off the oxygen supply, leading to rapid flame suppression.
- **Personal fire protection:** The blanket can be wrapped around a person to assist escape from a fire, acting as a barrier against heat and smoke.
- **Safe on electrical and oil fires:** Silicone does not conduct electricity, making the blanket suitable for electrical fires; it can also be used on oil and grease fires that cannot safely be extinguished with water.

### Self-Cooling Silicone Blanket
- **Heat absorption:** On contact with the body, the blanket absorbs heat and rapidly lowers skin temperature.
- **Thermal protection:** Intended for use during fires or in harsh weather conditions to help maintain a more comfortable body temperature for the user.

## 🎬 Practical Demonstration & Testing

Both prototypes were tested in practical, hands-on demonstrations:

- **Silicone fire blanket:** A small fire was ignited using flammable materials and was successfully extinguished using the blanket, which remained undamaged and unaffected by the exercise.
- **Self-cooling blanket:** Tested in a hot environment, where users reported a noticeable decrease in body temperature and a feeling of comfort and refreshment.

**Note on scope:** These were practical/informal demonstrations conducted as part of the graduation project, not third-party certified laboratory tests. No specific extinguishing times, certified temperature ratings, or official safety-standard test results are claimed here beyond what is described above.

## 🧯 Comparison: Silico Guard vs. Traditional Fiberglass Fire Blanket

The following comparison was developed as part of the project to position the silicone blanket against conventional fiberglass fire blankets, based on the material characteristics of silicone versus fiberglass:

| Feature | Silicone Fire Blanket (Silico Guard) | Traditional Fiberglass Blanket |
|---|---|---|
| Heat resistance | Withstands up to 1000°C or more | Withstands approx. 500–700°C |
| Flexibility & folding | Very flexible, easy to fold and store | Brittle; can crack over time |
| Reusability | Reusable multiple times | Limited; may be damaged after first use |
| Water & oil resistance | Fully resistant to water and oils | May absorb certain liquids |
| Chemical resistance | Resistant to strong chemicals | May react with some chemicals |
| Sticking to burnt material | Does not stick to burnt materials | Some materials may stick to it during a fire |
| Skin safety | Smooth and safe on skin | May cause irritation or sensitivity on contact |
| Durability / lifespan | Longer expected lifespan; more tear-resistant | Prone to damage and wear over time |
| Use in tight spaces | Flexible; adapts easily to small spaces | Can be difficult to apply in small spaces |
| Use in kitchens/vehicles | Unaffected by oils; well-suited for kitchens and cars | May be affected by oils and grease |
| Weight & portability | Lightweight, flexible, folds well | Lightweight but may break with repeated folding |

*Note: this table reflects the project's material-based comparison and design rationale, not independently certified lab test results.*

A formal, documented risk assessment was not conducted for this project. ## ✅ Safety Considerations

Based on the project's own design rationale:

- The silicone blanket is electrically non-conductive, making it suitable for electrical fires.
- The material is described as smooth and safe on skin, reducing irritation risk during use.
- The blanket is not affected by oils or greases, supporting safe use in kitchen/vehicle fire scenarios.
